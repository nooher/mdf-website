Registration with the Charity Commission for England and Wales came through on 22 May 2026, under number 1218194. Muft Development Foundation is constituted as a Charitable Incorporated Organisation.

Two things follow from that. The first is that the Foundation can receive donations as a charity. The second matters more: it is now answerable for what it does with them. Our accounts, our trustees and our governing document sit on the public register, and anyone can look them up.

The register entry and our constitution are both linked from the About page. We would rather you checked than took our word for it.

What registration does not mean is that work has already been delivered. It has not. This page will carry the reports when it has, including what each project cost.
