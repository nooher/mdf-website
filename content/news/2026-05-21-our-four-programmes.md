The constitution gives the Foundation four purposes, and we have kept the programmes to exactly those four rather than inventing more.

**Poverty relief** covers immediate material need — food, essential household costs, and support for households in hardship.

**Education** covers access to schooling and the costs that interrupt it, because most of the families we want to reach are held back by interrupted schooling rather than by a lack of effort.

**Youth development** covers young people who have left school and have no clear next step.

**Islamic education** covers teaching and the materials that go with it.

Each programme page sets out what the work involves. Where a programme has not yet funded anything, the page says so. We would rather leave a page thin than pad it out with intentions.
