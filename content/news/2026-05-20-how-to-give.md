The simplest way to give is a bank transfer straight to the Foundation's account. The details are on the Donate page, with a reference to quote so the gift can be matched to you.

A standing order does the same thing on a repeating basis, and is the most useful kind of gift for planning, because it tells us what we can commit to.

**Zakat** is kept separate and is only spent on purposes that qualify for it. Mark the transfer as Zakat and it will be recorded that way.

For a larger gift, or if you want to discuss what it would be used for before you send it, write to the donations address on the Donate page and a trustee will reply.

One warning. Our bank details change only when we publish a change here and on the Donate page. If you ever receive an email or a message telling you the account has changed, treat it as fraud and contact us on the published address before sending anything.
