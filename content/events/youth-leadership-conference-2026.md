A conference for youth, parents and supporters, fostering leadership grounded in Islamic values. It is **open to all faiths and backgrounds**.

The day is built around the four purposes the Foundation exists for: poverty relief, education, youth development and Islamic education. We expect between 150 and 200 people.

**What to expect**

- Workshops
- Discussions
- Networking
- Inspiration

Entry is free, but places must be booked in advance using the registration form.
